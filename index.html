Pune to Patna, three days on the road

About 1,850 to 1,900 km and 30 to 33 hours of actual driving. Split it with two night stops and drive in daylight only.

The route

  1. PuneLeave by 6 AM. Shirdi, then onto the Samruddhi expressway.
  2. Nagpur Day 1 · ~700 km · 9 to 10 hFastest leg. Night stop 1. Fill the tank.
  3. Varanasi Day 2 · ~580 km · ~10 hSeoni, Jabalpur, Katni, Rewa on NH44 and NH30. Some two-lane stretches. Night stop 2. Petrol is cheapest in UP, so fill here.
  4. Patna Day 3 · ~260 km · 5 to 6 hMohania, Sasaram, Arrah, Koilwar bridge.
December fog

Early December brings patchy fog from 5 to 9 AM, worst between Rewa and Patna. Dense fog runs late December to mid January. Drive 8 AM to 5 PM only in UP and Bihar, keep to 30 km/h or less in thick fog, use low beam and fog lamps, and stop at a fuel station, never on the shoulder.

Tirupati and Tirumala, three days by train

Overnight trains reach Renigunta, 10 km from Tirupati, in about 15 hours. Driving is ~1,090 km each way with a Hyderabad stop, which eats four days for a three-day trip.

Day plan

  1. D1

    Arrive Renigunta around 7:30 AM on 22159. Check in and rest. Evening: Sri Padmavathi temple at Tiruchanur, then Govindaraja Swamy temple.

  2. D2

    Up to Tirumala by TTD bus (Rs 60 to 100) or cab (Rs 1,200 to 1,500 return). Darshan, laddus, then Akasa Ganga and Papavinasam. Back down by evening.

  3. D3

    Srikalahasti (36 km) or Kanipakam, then the night train home.

Book darshan 90 days ahead

Rs 300 Special Entry tickets are online only at ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in, released at 10 AM, six per login, and festival dates sell out in minutes. Under 5s go free. Babies under 1 get free priority darshan through the Supadam entrance, 12 to 6 PM, with parents only and the birth certificate.

  • TTD rooms: Vishnu Nivasam opposite the station Rs 300 to 1,300, Srinivasam Rs 200 to 600.
  • Free Sarva Darshan is the fallback, with 6 to 18 hour waits at peak.
  • Avoid Vaikuntha Ekadasi (20 to 29 Dec 2026), Brahmotsavam and Sankranti.

Goa, four days by car

460 km on NH48 through Satara, Kolhapur and Belgaum: about 9 to 10 hours and Rs 605 in tolls each way. With a family, the car beats Goa taxis.

Day plan, based in South Goa

  1. D1

    Leave Pune at 6 AM, reach around 4 PM. Sunset at Colva or Benaulim.

  2. D2

    Old Goa churches, a Fontainhas walk in Panjim, Miramar, and an evening Mandovi cruise (Rs 400 to 500 a head).

  3. D3

    Palolem or Agonda and Cabo de Rama fort. Or a North day: Aguada, Candolim, Anjuna market on Saturdays.

  4. D4

    Head home by 8 AM. The Belgaum to Kolhapur stretch is quick.

  • South Goa (Colva, Benaulim, Palolem) is quiet and suits small kids and parents. North Goa is markets and nightlife.
  • Staying 1 to 2 km inland saves 30 to 40%. A villa with a kitchen makes home cooking easy.
  • Tolls one way: Khed 95, Satara 70, Kagal 110, Belgaum 155, Londa 85, Mollem 90.

Maharashtra, four circuits

Costs are mid-range for 4 adults by car, with food mostly home cooked.

Start here

Shirdi, Nashik, Trimbakeshwar

Rs 12,400

2 days · 560 km. Flat, easy roads and a good first long run for a new car. Shirdi darshan is free or Rs 200 paid; Kakad aarti Rs 600.

Best road trip

Konkan coast into Goa

Rs 51,000

5 to 6 days · ~1,150 km. Ganpatipule, Ratnagiri, Tarkarli and Malvan, then Goa. Down the coast on NH66, back fast on NH48. Budget version about Rs 32,000.

Ajanta, Ellora, Daulatabad

Rs 21,000

3 days · 760 km. Ajanta is closed Mondays and Ellora Tuesdays. Ajanta has steep steps and heat, so it is hard with a baby carrier or anyone with balance trouble.

Mahabaleshwar, Panchgani

Rs 10,500 to 12,500

1 to 2 days · 120 km, Rs 215 tolls. Strawberry season starts in December. Midweek rooms Rs 2,500 to 4,000, weekends double.

A sensible order for the season

Built around a fixed early-December Patna drive.

November

Shirdi and Nashik over a weekend. Easy roads to get used to the car.

7 to 9 December

Pune to Patna by car. Leave on the 7th to arrive on the 9th. Come back before about 25 December, or by train, before the dense fog sets in.

Late December

Avoid Tirumala (Vaikuntha Ekadasi crowds) and Goa (peak pricing).

Mid to late January

Konkan coast and Goa road trip. Prices drop after 3 January and the weather is at its best.

February

Tirupati by train. Book Rs 300 darshan around 24 November at 10 AM.
